## Slide 11

What we learned

Treat them like interns

Ask your Grok Bots to do their homework, study new areas, and learn from other engineers. No skill invocation or long prompts required. Simply chat.

Copilots

When your Bot needs human on some tasks repeatedly, you should think about how to automate the flow rather than keep manually unblocking them.

AI teammates

To ensure a comprehensive feedback loop, consider how your Bot can observe and receive signals about the work being done.

### Speaker notes

Full organizer-supplied judging rubric. Each criterion is scored 1-5. No weighting or tie-break rule was supplied.
Organizer clarification: Grok Bot and/or SpaceXAI APIs are required. The slide summary reflects that clarification; the rubric below preserves the wording supplied.

WORKING PROTOTYPE
The submission must run end-to-end and demonstrate the agent’s core functionality.
1) Code does not run at all.
2) Major crashes or missing core features.
3) Partial functionality, major bugs remain.
4) Mostly stable with minor bugs.
5) Fully functional, polished, and ready for demo.

TECHNICAL COMPLEXITY & INTEGRATION
Depth of Grok Bot usage, tool-use APIs, and integration supporting collaboration between people and AI or across multiple agents.
1) Simple static mock-up.
2) Basic AI interaction without meaningful integration.
3) Functional integration supporting human-AI or agent-to-agent collaboration.
4) Sophisticated collaboration using tools, shared context, or coordinated tasks.
5) Advanced collaborative workflows with robust error handling and real-world data integration.

INNOVATION & CREATIVITY
Novelty of the agent concept, clever problem framing, and original solution design.
1) Replicates an existing demo.
2) Minor tweak of known idea.
3) Useful but not surprising.
4) Creative twist or new application of known tech.
5) Groundbreaking agent that opens a new use-case or paradigm.

REAL-WORLD IMPACT
Potential to solve a tangible problem, user value, scalability, and deployment readiness.
1) Purely academic or toy example.
2) Interesting but limited user relevance.
3) Solves a niche need.
4) Addresses a clear market or social need.
5) Demonstrates life-changing or large-scale impact potential (e.g., accessibility, productivity gains).

THEME ALIGNMENT
How well the project embodies the hackathon’s focus on Multiplayer AI or Multi-agent Systems built with Grok Bot.
1) Barely related to either track.
2) Superficial connection without a functional collaborative demo.
3) Demonstrates basic human-AI or agent-to-agent collaboration.
4) Clear implementation where collaboration is central to the project.
5) Exemplifies either track, with people and AI or multiple agents working together cohesively toward a shared goal.
